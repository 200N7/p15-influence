4. OBSERVATION
--------------
The three scenarios demonstrate different operating conditions while
using the same integrated application.

Normal:
Low cross-community propagation and clearly separated communities.

Viral shock:
Influence probability and network capacity increase sharply, while
community structure reorganizes because of stronger cross-community ties.

Stress:
The same algorithms remain operational on a deterministic 500-user,
2000-relationship network.

The stress dataset is reproducible using:
bench/generate_stress.c
